Rules & Payouts

European roulette uses a single-zero wheel with 37 pockets (0–36). The ball's landing pocket decides every bet on the table. The house edge on every bet is 2.70% (1 in 37).

How a round works

  1. Waiting for host — the first player seated is the host. Once two or more players are seated, the host presses Start Match.
  2. Betting open (30 s) — pick a chip and click spots on the felt. When every player presses Lock Bets, betting closes immediately. The host can also close bets early.
  3. Spinning — the server draws the winning pocket with cryptographic randomness and the wheel spins for everyone at the same time.
  4. Payout — winning chips stay on the felt and are paid; losing chips are swept off. Bankrolls update.
  5. Round summary — a 5-second recap, then betting reopens.

A winning bet returns your stake plus the payout — e.g. $5 straight up on a winner returns $180 ($175 win + $5 stake). Zero loses every outside bet.

Table guidelines

  • Every player starts with $100. Chips are deducted the moment you place them and refunded if you remove them.
  • Chips come in $1, $5, $10, $25 and $100. You can bet on as many spots as you like; the spot limit is $1,000.
  • Aim between numbers to place inside bets: hover a line for a split, an intersection for a corner, the bottom edge for a street or six line. The highlighted numbers show exactly what a spot covers.
  • Each player's chips carry their colour and badge. When several players share a spot, the chips cluster — hover to see who wagered what.
  • Locked bets can't be changed. Unlock to edit them while the timer is still running.
  • If you drop out, your seat is held for 45 seconds — reload the page to reclaim it. If every bankroll empties, the host can start a fresh match.
